# Change Request: CR-2025-08-09-04 — Portfolio Keep-or-Sell Review for Existing Holdings

Date: 2025-08-09 22:16 CEST
Author: Cascade (Senior Trusted Financial Advisor)
Status: Proposed
Scope: Evaluate each existing ETF and Stock from CSV lists and decide KEEP or SELL, suggesting better alternatives when warranted. Integrate with existing crews (Stock, ETF) and keep final reporting agent tool-less.

---

[TOC]

## Objectives

- Provide an automated, evidence-based review of current holdings listed in:
  - `data/etf.csv` (schema: `Name,Ticker,Currency`)
  - `data/stock.csv` (schema: `Name,Ticker,Currency`)
- For each holding, output a decision: KEEP or SELL.
- When SELL is recommended, include up to 3 higher-quality alternatives surfaced by our screeners with rationale.
- Preserve FinWiz design rules: tool-less final reporter, HTML-first output, standardized risk and data contracts.

---

## Recommended Actions

- __[Input ingestion & validation]__
  - Implement a small ingestion layer that reads `data/etf.csv` and `data/stock.csv`.
  - Normalize `Ticker` by stripping the `Yahoo:` prefix and validating existence against Yahoo Finance (reuse/extend strict validation from CR-2025-08-09-03).
  - Emit a normalized list with fields: `asset_class` (stock|etf), `name`, `ticker`, `currency`.

- __[Per-holding analysis via existing crews]__
  - For each stock holding, invoke Stock crew tasks used in screening:
    - 10-K synthesis and citations (`ten_k_insights[]`).
    - Market sentiment aggregation (`market_sentiment`).
    - Risk assessment with standardized scale (`risk_score_standardized`).
  - For each ETF holding, invoke ETF crew tasks:
    - Factsheet highlights (expense ratio, top holdings, replication, issuer, as_of).
    - Risk assessment with standardized scale (`risk_score_standardized`).
  - Ensure parity with CR-2025-08-09-01/02/03 contracts and schemas.

- __[Composite scoring & decision policy]__
  - Compute a `composite_score` per holding combining:
    - Quality: fundamentals (10-K excerpts for stocks, factsheet metrics for ETFs).
    - Sentiment: normalized mean score.
    - Risk: prefer lower risk for comparable return; cap overweight risk.
    - Cost (ETFs): favor lower expense ratio when exposures are similar.
  - Decision thresholds:
    - KEEP if `composite_score >= keep_threshold` AND no materially superior alternatives.
    - SELL if there exists an alternative with `alt_score >= composite_score + delta_threshold` AND `risk_diff <= max_risk_step`.
  - Default params (feature-flag tunable): `keep_threshold=0.55`, `delta_threshold=0.10`, `max_risk_step=1`.

- __[Alternatives ("other suggestions")]__
  - Source candidates from our existing screeners in Stock and ETF crews (same run), already ranked by their own `composite_score`.
  - Filter by asset class, theme/exposure similarity (sector/region for stocks where applicable; index/exposure for ETFs), currency compatibility.
  - Return top 3 alternatives with tickers, short thesis bullets, risk score, and key metrics (e.g., expense ratio for ETFs).

- __[Diversification & suitability checks]__
  - Add a lightweight portfolio-level pass:
    - Detect concentration by sector/region/issuer (ETFs) and mega-cap overweights (stocks).
    - Avoid SELL->BUY suggestions that worsen concentration or exceed risk budget.
  - Respect the 1,000 CHF/month investor profile. Favor DCA-friendly, liquid, diversified suggestions for SELL replacements.

- __[Reporter integration (tool-less)]__
  - Extend `src/finwiz/crews/report_crew/config/tasks.yaml` to add a new section in the final HTML: “Revue du portefeuille: Conserver ou Vendre”.
  - The final reporter consumes upstream context only (no tools), per rule and prior CRs. Output must follow `docs/output_formatting_guide.md` (HTML with emojis and sections).

- __[Contracts & schemas]__
  - Define a strict Pydantic v2 schema for the portfolio review output (align with CR-2025-08-09-02):
    - `PortfolioReview`: `as_of`, `base_currency`, `holdings[]`.
    - `HoldingDecision`: `asset_class`, `name`, `ticker`, `currency`, `decision` (KEEP|SELL), `composite_score`, `risk_score_standardized`, `rationale_bullets[]`, `citations[]`, `alternatives[]` (when SELL).
    - `Alternative`: `ticker`, `name`, `asset_class`, `composite_score`, `risk_score_standardized`, `key_metrics{}` (e.g., `expense_ratio`), `thesis_bullets[]`, `citations[]`.
  - Emit JSON-like structured context for the reporter; keep reporter template HTML-driven.

- __[Feature flags & config]__
  - `PORTFOLIO_REVIEW_ENABLED=true|false` (default true).
  - Thresholds configurable via env or YAML: `KEEP_THRESHOLD`, `DELTA_THRESHOLD`, `MAX_RISK_STEP`.
  - CSV paths configurable via env (`PORTFOLIO_ETF_CSV`, `PORTFOLIO_STOCK_CSV`) with defaults to `data/etf.csv` and `data/stock.csv`.

- __[Performance & caching]__
  - Reuse short-lived cache for news/search/SEC calls (30–60 min TTL) to control cost and latency.
  - Run per-holding analyses concurrently; preserve sequential final reporter.

## Acceptance Criteria

- __[Ingestion]__ CSVs are read and normalized; invalid tickers are flagged and do not crash the run.
- __[Decisions]__ Each holding receives KEEP or SELL with a `composite_score` and `rationale_bullets[]`; SELLs include up to 3 alternatives with supporting metrics.
- __[Reporter]__ Final HTML includes “Revue du portefeuille: Conserver ou Vendre”, preserving tool-less reporter behavior and formatting per `docs/output_formatting_guide.md`.
- __[Contracts]__ Portfolio review objects validate against strict Pydantic schemas; reporter consumes them without schema errors.
- __[Testing]__ `uv run pytest` passes by default; integration tests pass with `-m integration`.

---

## Risks & Mitigations

- __[Data quality / Ticker drift]__ Inconsistent tickers or delistings.
  - Mitigation: strict Yahoo validation with graceful degradation and actionable warnings.
- __[Over-trading / Churn]__ Excessive SELL recommendations due to small deltas.
  - Mitigation: sensible `delta_threshold`; require comparable risk; respect diversification checks.
- __[Schema drift]__ Upstream crew output keys may evolve.
  - Mitigation: contract tests, versioned schema keys in docs.
- __[Latency/Cost]__ Running analyses for all holdings.
  - Mitigation: caching, concurrency, and batched calls.
